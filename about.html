About ChemLab Assistant

AI-powered chemistry education for everyone

Mission

The Chemistry Laboratory Safety & Compound Assistant is an AI-powered RAG chatbot designed to help students, teachers, laboratory technicians, and chemistry enthusiasts learn about laboratory safety, chemical compounds, elements, acids, bases, salts, reactions, laboratory equipment, and basic analytical chemistry.

How It Works
Knowledge Base
Curated chemistry Q&A from verified educational sources
RAG Retrieval
Semantic search finds the most relevant context for your question
AI Generation
LLM generates accurate, educational responses grounded in retrieved knowledge
Safety Notice

This chatbot is intended for educational purposes only. It explains chemistry concepts, properties, safety precautions, and laboratory practices. It does not provide instructions that facilitate hazardous experiments or unsafe chemical procedures.
